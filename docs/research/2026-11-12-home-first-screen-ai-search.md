# Home page first screen: AI search (2026-11-12)

Why: the home page sold the rerun proof but not the AI-search job, while the
demand that makes a new SEO product win in 2025-2026 is AI search. The change is
copy and order only. No ranking, traffic or AI-citation guarantee is made.

## What the others promise on their first screen

Fetched 2026-11-12, plain `curl`, live pages only.

- ChatSEO (<https://chatseo.app/>): H1 "The SEO expert that makes you visible on
  Google", alternate line "The SEO expert that gets you customers from Google".
  Input "Enter your website.", CTA "Analyze my website". TrustMRR's listing
  (<https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatseo>) sells it as "The SEO expert that gets
  you #1 on Google and ChatGPT". Promise: a done-for-you expert that grows
  traffic and revenue.
- Rank Prompt (<https://rankprompt.com/>): H1 "Track and improve your AI search
  visibility". Sub-line: "Find out if ChatGPT recommends your business, and what
  to change so it does." It names ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude,
  Gemini, Muse and Grok, and offers "Analyze free". Promise: monitoring plus a
  change list across answer engines.
- Peec AI (<https://peec.ai/>): H1 "AI search analytics for marketing teams".
  Sub-line: "Track, analyze, and improve brand performance on AI search
  platforms" through "Visibility, Position, and Sentiment". Promise: a
  measurement platform for marketing teams.

All three promise visibility inside answer engines. SEO Fix Kit does not sample
answer engines or monitor citations. The honest promise is narrower and still
answers the same buyer question: whether Google and the AI crawlers behind
ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can read the pages, with proof for each problem,
the fix, and a rerun that confirms it. "Checks its own work" is the differentiator
the trackers do not have.
